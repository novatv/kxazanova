KXAZANOVA is the afro house & electronic project of Venezuelan-born, Amsterdam-based DJ and producer Casanova Beatz. Raised on Caribbean rhythm and shaped by the club culture of the Netherlands, his sound crosses tribal percussion with the deep, hypnotic groove of European house.

As a producer he has released on nine independent labels across Europe and the Americas — including a collaboration with international hitmaker Burak Yeter (“Nitrogen”, Connection Records). His biggest record, “Bumbata” (334,000+ streams), is a collaboration with Spanish afro house heavyweight Aaron Sevilla, released on AFRODISE — one of the genre’s leading labels.

His latest release, “Soca” (Make The Girls Dance Records, 2025), teams him up with Aaar, DJ ORELHA MPC and MC Theuzyn. Under his previous alias he has performed across 19 countries in Europe and Latin America, with festival appearances including EXIT Festival (Serbia), Latin Village and Pal Mundo (NL), Sea Dance (Montenegro) and Sea Star (Croatia).

A veteran of the Dutch club circuit, he has played venues across the Netherlands for over a decade. His catalog spans 30+ releases with 20,000+ monthly listeners on Spotify across more than 30 countries.
